HOW TO
KICK BACK.

  1. 01
    Aim

    Move your mouse or finger. Point opposite the direction you want to travel.

  2. 02
    Fire once

    Click or tap. Shoot down to rise, right to move left, and diagonally to arc.

  3. 03
    Make contact

    You only carry one bullet. Touch any pale platform or wall to reload.

  4. 04
    Climb

    Chain careful landings to reach the summit. There are no checkpoints—and no movement keys.
